# Wandernaut | Activity Discovery & Planning App 🚀

### Description

A local activity and hangout discovery/planning app that helps you find fun things to do and build your outing, all while staying within a desired budget

📍 **V1 Location Scope: Indianapolis**

Live Link 🔗: [TBE]

### Problem Statement

**_Finding something fun to do can take more planning than it should._**

❌ Activities, events, and costs are scattered throughout different platforms, making it difficult to explore what's nearby while knowing what an outing may cost.

**Goal ✅**: Encourage people to explore by bringing together budgeting, local discovery, and outing planning with a fun, approachable and intuitive app - _Wandernaut_.

## Key Features 🔑

**Local Discovery 🔍** - Explore nearby places and events through an interactive map.

**Explore by Interest 💡** - Filter activites and destinations by category to tailor to your plans.

**Budget Planning 💵** - See estimated costs of what you may spend at each venue/event as you curate your outing, keeping track of your outing total.

**Save for Later 💾** - Bookmark your plan for repeat adventures or saving your place for later
